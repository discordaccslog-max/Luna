1. Cookies set by this website: none

We do not set cookies on this website — not strictly necessary ones, not functional ones, not analytics ones and not advertising ones. We also do not use localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, web beacons, pixels, fingerprinting, or any equivalent technology to identify or follow you.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser’s developer tools, go to the storage or application tab, and load any page on this site. The cookie list stays empty.

2. Why there is no cookie banner

Cookie consent banners exist because a website wants to store or read information on your device for purposes that are not strictly necessary. This site does not do that, so under the UK and EU ePrivacy rules there is no consent to collect and no banner to show.

If we ever introduce analytics or any other non-essential storage, we will add a proper consent mechanism — one that asks before anything is set, and that lets you decline as easily as accept — and we will update this notice first.

3. No third-party content is loaded

Every file this website requests is served from this domain. There are no third-party fonts (the site uses your device’s own system fonts), no embedded videos, no social media widgets, no chat widgets, no advertising networks and no tag managers.

This means no third party can observe your visit to this website, because no third party is contacted during it.

4. Cookies at checkout (Whop)

When you click a purchase button you leave this website and continue on Whop’s own site. Whop is an independent company and sets its own cookies on its own domain, typically for signing you in, keeping your checkout session, preventing fraud, and its own analytics.

Those cookies are outside our control and are not set by us. They are governed by Whop’s privacy policy and by whatever consent choices Whop presents to you on its pages. Please read them before completing a purchase.

5. Hosting logs

Our host, Vercel, processes standard server request data in order to serve the site and keep it secure. This is server-side logging, not a cookie, and is described in section 3 of the Privacy Policy.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

Although this site sets none, you can control cookies generally through your browser settings — blocking them, deleting them, or being warned before one is set. Blocking cookies will not affect this website in any way, but it may affect Whop’s checkout, which does need its own cookies in order to process your payment.
