Delivery Policy

Everything we sell is a digital download. Nothing is posted, nothing is shipped, and no delivery address is ever required.

1. Digital delivery only

StarFN Competitive & Kovaaks Coaching sells downloadable PDF files. There is no physical product, no packaging, no courier and no shipping charge. You will never be asked for a postal address, and we will never add a delivery fee to your order.

2. How and when you receive your Product

Your download is released as soon as your payment clears. Delivery is handled by Whop, our payment and fulfilment provider:

  • a receipt and access link are sent to the email address you entered at checkout
  • the same Product is available in your Whop account, under your purchases

In practice this takes under a minute. If a payment is held for manual review by the processor — which happens occasionally with new cards or unusual billing locations — delivery may take up to 24 hours.

4. Re-downloads and access period

Your access does not expire. You can download your Product again as many times as you like from your Whop account, on any device, at no extra cost.

If we publish a corrected or updated version of an edition you already own, the current version is what you will get when you re-download it. We do not promise a particular update schedule.

We would still recommend saving a personal backup copy once you have downloaded it.

5. If your download has not arrived

Work through these steps first — they resolve the large majority of cases:

  1. Check the spam, junk and promotions folders of the email address you used at checkout.
  2. Search your inbox for “Whop” rather than for our business name.
  3. Sign in to your Whop account using exactly the email address you checked out with — the Product will be listed under your purchases.
  4. Check whether you mistyped your email address at checkout.
  5. Wait up to 24 hours in case the payment is still being reviewed.

If none of that works, email starfnshop@gmail.com with your Whop order or receipt reference and the email address you used. We will verify the order and deliver the file manually, normally well within 2 business days. If we cannot deliver it at all, you get a full refund under the Refund Policy.

6. If the file will not open

The Products are standard PDF files and open in any modern browser or PDF reader. If a file appears damaged, tell us which device, browser or reader you used and what you saw, and we will supply a replacement copy. If we cannot provide a working file, you are entitled to a full refund.

7. Your responsibilities

Please make sure the email address you enter at checkout is correct and can receive mail from Whop. We can only deliver to the address associated with the order. We are not responsible for delivery failures caused by an incorrect address, a full mailbox, or an email filter on your side — though we will always help you sort it out if you contact us.

Questions about this policy

Email starfnshop@gmail.com and we will respond within 2 business days. If your question relates to a purchase, please include the order reference from your Whop receipt.
