Overview

This is the full written coaching playbook. It takes the way a tournament player actually thinks — read the rules, pick a game plan, drop with a route, rotate on time, survive the mid-game, then execute the endgame — and lays it out as something you can read once and then apply deliberately, game after game.

It is deliberately a document rather than a video course. You can re-read a page mid-session, keep the checklists open on a second screen, and come back to the practice schedule in two weeks without scrubbing through hours of footage to find the one thing you needed.

The Full edition contains everything in the Lite edition, expanded, plus the chapters on mid-game survival, the mental game, the VOD self-review method, the structured KovaaK’s routine and the 14-day practice schedule.

What is inside

  1. Reading the format before you queue. How tournament point systems actually work across Cash Cups, FNCS formats and divisional play — placement points versus elimination points, the scoring thresholds that matter, and why the same lobby rewards completely different behaviour depending on the rules page.
  2. Placement-first versus elimination-first. Choosing a game plan per point system and per region, and recognising the specific moments where a fight is worth the risk and where it simply is not.
  3. Drop selection and loot routing. Contested versus uncontested drops, building a repeatable loot route, hitting your first rotate on time, and what to do when your spot is taken.
  4. Rotation and zone reading. Early, mid and late rotation timing, reading the zone pull, taking the right side of the circle, and the real cost of rotating late.
  5. Mid-game survival. Material economy, avoiding third parties, when to hold height and when to give it up, and how to disengage from a fight you have already won on points.
  6. Endgame. Final circles, moving with the zone, box-fight and trade discipline, surge requirements and how to meet them without throwing your placement.
  7. Loadout and inventory. Slot priorities, heal splits, mobility, and the inventory decisions that quietly decide endgames.
  8. Mental game and tilt control. Queue scheduling, reset routines between games, and reviewing a bad session without spiralling.
  9. The VOD self-review method. A repeatable checklist for watching your own replays and turning them into one specific thing to fix next session.
  10. Aim and mechanics with KovaaK’s. How to structure a weekly routine, the difference between a warm-up block and a training block, keeping sensitivity consistent across the aim trainer and the game, and how to tell whether a routine is actually working.
  11. The 14-day practice schedule. A day-by-day plan combining aim training, scrims and tournament queueing, plus quick-reference checklists you can keep open on a second screen.
