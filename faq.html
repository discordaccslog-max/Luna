Frequently asked questions

Delivery, refunds, devices, licensing, and the things we are careful not to promise. If your question is not here, email us before you buy.

Is this a one-time payment or a subscription?

One-time. You pay once for the edition you choose and the PDF is yours to keep and re-download. There is no recurring charge, no trial that converts, and nothing to cancel.

How quickly do I get the PDF?

Immediately. The download is released as soon as your payment clears, through your Whop receipt and your Whop account. In the rare case that a payment is held for review by the processor, allow up to 24 hours. If nothing has arrived after that, email us and we will deliver the file manually — see the Delivery Policy.

What is the difference between the Lite and Full editions?

The Lite edition is a 5-page condensed version of the core placement framework for $29.99. The Full edition is the complete 10+ page playbook for $49.99, and adds the rotation, mid-game, endgame and mental-game chapters, the VOD self-review method, the structured KovaaK’s routine and the 14-day practice schedule. There is a line-by-line comparison on the products page.

Can I upgrade from Lite to Full later?

There is no automatic upgrade path at the moment — the two editions are sold separately. If you buy the Lite edition and then decide you want the Full one, email us before purchasing and we will tell you exactly where you stand rather than letting you pay twice for overlapping material.

What device do I need?

Anything that opens a PDF: a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop, using a built-in PDF reader or any modern web browser. No special software, no game and no hardware purchase is needed to read the guide.

Can I re-download it if I lose the file?

Yes, as many times as you like, from your Whop account. There is no expiry and no download limit. If you cannot get into your Whop account, email us from the address you used at checkout.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, in the circumstances set out in the Refund Policy: non-delivery, a corrupt or unopenable file, a duplicate charge, or a material mismatch between what the product page describes and what you received. Because the PDF is delivered to you instantly, the statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for digital content does not survive delivery — you confirm that at checkout. We would much rather answer a question before you buy, so please ask.

No, and we will never say otherwise. Competitive Fortnite is a skill game played against other improving players. These playbooks explain decision-making and practice structure; your results depend on your own practice, hardware, connection, region and opponents. See the Disclaimer.

Do I need to own KovaaK’s to use the aim routine?

To follow the routine as written, yes — KovaaK’s is a separate product sold by a third party and is not included in your purchase. The underlying principles (warm-up versus training blocks, consistent sensitivity, measuring progress) transfer to other aim trainers, but the specific scenario structure assumes KovaaK’s.

No. There are no cheats, exploits, macros, scripts or account services anywhere in these guides, and we do not sell them. Everything described is ordinary in-game decision-making and practice. You remain responsible for following the Epic Games Terms of Service and the official rules of any tournament you enter.
