About StarFN Competitive & Kovaaks Coaching

An independent, one-person coaching business that publishes written Fortnite competitive guidance and aim-training structure as downloadable PDFs.

Why written coaching

Most Fortnite coaching is live: you book a slot, you share a screen, you get an hour, and three weeks later you cannot remember half of it. That works, but it is expensive, it is hard to schedule around school or work, and it does not leave you with anything to go back to.

These playbooks exist for the other way of learning. The decision-making that separates a player who finishes 40th from one who scores points is not secret, but it is specific, and it is much easier to apply when it is written down in order: read the rules, pick a game plan, drop with a route, rotate on time, survive the mid-game, execute the endgame. You can re-read a page between matches. You can print the checklist. You can come back to the practice schedule in two weeks.

The aim-training half

The “Kovaaks” part of the name is about practice structure. Raw aim is only one input into a tournament result, but it is the one players most often train badly — long unfocused sessions, a sensitivity that changes every week, and no way to tell whether any of it is working. The Full playbook includes a structured weekly routine built around KovaaK’s, with the distinction between a warm-up block and an actual training block, and guidance on keeping sensitivity consistent between the aim trainer and the game. The Lite playbook includes a short starter warm-up.

KovaaK’s is a separate product published by a third party. You need your own copy to follow the routine, and buying our playbook does not include it.
