Accessibility Statement

We want this website to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or reduced-motion settings.

1. Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA across this website. Accessibility is treated as part of building the site, not as an afterthought, and every new page is checked against the same standard.

2. What we have done

  • Semantic HTML throughout, with correct landmark regions (header, navigation, main, footer) and a logical heading order on every page
  • A skip link as the first focusable element, so keyboard users can jump straight past the navigation to the main content
  • Full keyboard operability — every link, button and expandable section can be reached and operated with a keyboard alone, in a sensible order
  • Visible focus indicators with a high-contrast outline that is never removed
  • Colour contrast of body text, headings and interactive elements tested against the WCAG AA thresholds, with colour never used as the only way of conveying meaning
  • Resizable text — the layout uses relative units and remains usable at 200% zoom without horizontal scrolling or loss of content
  • Reduced motion support — the site honours the prefers-reduced-motion setting and disables smooth scrolling and transitions
  • Responsive layout that works from small phones to large desktop displays
  • Descriptive link text rather than bare “click here” links
  • Accessible tables, with proper header cells and scopes on the product comparison
  • A genuinely light page — no third-party scripts, no autoplaying media, no pop-ups, no interstitials and no cookie banner to trap focus
  • Graceful degradation — all content and navigation remain readable with JavaScript disabled

3. Accessibility of the PDFs

Our Products are PDF documents. PDFs vary in how well they work with assistive technology, and we are continuing to improve ours.

If you use a screen reader or other assistive technology and have trouble with a PDF you have bought, email starfnshop@gmail.com. We will provide the content in an alternative accessible format — such as plain text, accessible HTML, or a large-print version — at no extra cost. If we cannot provide a usable alternative, you are entitled to a full refund.

4. Accessibility of checkout

Checkout takes place on Whop’s website, which we do not build or control, so we cannot warrant its accessibility. If you hit a barrier during checkout, contact us — we will help you complete your purchase another way and we will raise the issue with Whop.

5. Known limitations

We are aware of the following and are working on them:

  • Some of our PDF files are not yet fully tagged for screen-reader navigation. Accessible alternatives are available on request, as described above.
  • Third-party pages we link to, including Whop’s checkout, are outside our control.

If you encounter something not listed here, please tell us — we would rather know.

6. Feedback and how to reach us

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of this website. Email starfnshop@gmail.com with “Accessibility” in the subject line and tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology and browser you were using. We aim to respond within 2 business days and to fix confirmed barriers as quickly as we reasonably can.

If you are not satisfied with our response, please tell us — we will escalate it internally and keep you updated rather than letting it lapse.

7. Assessment and review

This statement is based on our own evaluation of the website against WCAG 2.1 Level AA, using a combination of automated tooling, keyboard-only testing and manual review. It has not been independently audited by a third party. We review this statement whenever the site changes substantially.

Questions about this policy

Email starfnshop@gmail.com and we will respond within 2 business days. If your question relates to a purchase, please include the order reference from your Whop receipt.
