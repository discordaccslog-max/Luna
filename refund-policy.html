1. Scope

This policy applies to every Product sold by StarFN Competitive & Kovaaks Coaching and forms part of our Terms of Service. It applies alongside, and never instead of, any mandatory consumer-protection right you have where you live.

2. The nature of what you are buying

Our Products are downloadable PDF files, not physical goods and not a service delivered over time. Access is released the moment your payment clears. Once a file has been downloaded it cannot be returned in any meaningful sense, which is why the rules below are structured the way they are.

3. Statutory right of withdrawal (UK, EU and similar jurisdictions)

Consumers in the UK and the EU normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase without giving a reason. For digital content supplied immediately, that right can be waived, and the law requires us to tell you so clearly before you pay.

By completing checkout you expressly request that we supply the digital content to you immediately, and you acknowledge that you will lose your 14-day right of withdrawal once the download has been made available to you. This is stated next to every purchase button on this website and is confirmed at checkout.

If, exceptionally, the download was not made available to you, your withdrawal right has not been lost and you may cancel for a full refund under section 4 below.

Waiving the withdrawal right does not waive your rights in respect of faulty or misdescribed digital content. Those rights survive, and are covered in section 4.

4. When we will always refund you in full

No questions, no argument. Contact us and we will refund the full amount you paid if:

  • Non-delivery — your payment went through but the download was never made available to you, and we cannot deliver it within a reasonable time of you telling us
  • Corrupt or unopenable file — the PDF will not open or is damaged, and we cannot supply a working replacement
  • Duplicate charge — you were charged more than once for the same Product
  • Wrong Product — you received a different edition from the one you paid for and we cannot correct it
  • Material misdescription — the Product differs materially from what its product page described (for example, a stated chapter is simply absent)
  • Unauthorised purchase — the payment was made without the account holder’s authority, including a purchase made by a child without a parent or guardian’s consent

In these cases we will usually offer to fix the problem first — by re-sending the file, supplying a repaired copy, or swapping you to the correct edition — but the choice of a refund instead is yours, and we will not make you accept a replacement.

5. When a refund is not available

Because the Product is delivered instantly and in full, we cannot offer refunds simply because:

  • you have read the guide and changed your mind about buying it
  • you already knew some or all of the material (the complete contents of each edition are listed on its product page before you pay)
  • you bought the Lite edition and then decided you wanted the Full edition, or vice versa
  • you did not achieve a particular placement, qualification, rank or amount of prize money — we never promise these, as explained in the Disclaimer
  • you did not apply the material, or did not have time to
  • you bought it by mistake but have already downloaded it

This is also why we would genuinely rather you ask first. Email us before you buy and we will tell you honestly whether an edition is right for you.

6. Goodwill refunds

Outside the situations in section 4, we may still issue a discretionary refund within 14 days of purchase where the circumstances clearly warrant it — for example an obvious accidental purchase reported promptly where the file has not been downloaded. This is a goodwill gesture decided case by case, not an entitlement, and it does not create a precedent or affect your statutory rights.

7. How to request a refund

Email starfnshop@gmail.com with “Refund request” in the subject line, and include:

  • the email address you used at checkout
  • your Whop order or receipt reference
  • which edition you bought
  • a short description of the problem — and, for a technical fault, the device, browser or PDF reader you tried and what you saw on screen

We acknowledge every refund request within 2 business days and aim to reach a decision within 5 business days. If we need more information we will say exactly what we need and why.

8. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued through Whop to your original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card, account or person.

Once we approve a refund, Whop processes it promptly; how quickly it appears on your statement is then up to your bank or card issuer, and is typically 5–10 business days. Any currency-conversion difference applied by your own bank is outside our control.

When a refund is issued, your licence to use the Product ends immediately and you must delete any copies you hold.

9. Chargebacks

If you believe something has gone wrong, please contact us first — almost everything is resolved within a few days, and a chargeback takes considerably longer for you than an email does.

Where a chargeback is raised, we will respond to the payment provider with the order record and the relevant correspondence. We reserve the right to decline future orders from an account that has raised a chargeback without first attempting to resolve the matter with us, and to treat a chargeback on a downloaded Product as a termination of the licence to use it.

10. If we cancel your order

If we cancel an order ourselves — for example because of a suspected fraudulent payment or a pricing error — you receive a full refund and we will explain why.

11. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes your rights under mandatory consumer-protection law in your country of residence, including rights relating to digital content that is not of satisfactory quality, not fit for purpose, or not as described. If this policy and that law ever conflict, that law prevails.

Questions about this policy

Email starfnshop@gmail.com and we will respond within 2 business days. If your question relates to a purchase, please include the order reference from your Whop receipt.
