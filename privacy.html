1. Who is responsible for your data

StarFN Competitive & Kovaaks Coaching is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us about any privacy matter at starfnshop@gmail.com.

2. What this website collects: nothing

Browsing this website does not create a profile of you. Specifically, this site:

  • sets no cookies of any kind — see the Cookie Notice
  • runs no analytics (no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Vercel Analytics, nothing)
  • contains no advertising or social media tracking pixels
  • loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or embedded content — every file the page requests comes from this domain, and the site uses your device’s own system fonts
  • has no contact form, no newsletter signup and no account system
  • does not use browser local storage or session storage

There is no tracking to opt out of, because there is no tracking.

3. Server logs

This website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel’s infrastructure processes technical request data — such as IP address, timestamp, requested URL and user-agent string — in order to deliver pages to you, keep the service secure and prevent abuse. We do not use these logs to build profiles, and we do not combine them with any other data.

Vercel acts as our hosting processor. You can read Vercel’s privacy policy at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. When you buy something

Purchases are handled entirely by Whop, our payment and delivery provider. When you check out, you provide your details to Whop, not to this website. Whop acts as an independent controller for the payment data it collects, and also processes order data on our behalf.

We never see your card number, CVV, or any other payment credential. Card data is entered on Whop’s hosted, PCI-compliant checkout and is never transmitted to or stored by us.

What we can see in our Whop dashboard, for the purpose of fulfilling and supporting your order, is limited to:

  • your name or username as supplied at checkout
  • the email address you used
  • which Product you bought, the amount, the currency and the date
  • an order or receipt reference
  • the country or region used for tax calculation
  • whether the order was refunded, disputed or charged back

Please read Whop’s privacy policy as well, as it governs what Whop does with your data, including any cookies Whop sets on its own domain during checkout.

5. When you email us

If you email our support address, we receive your email address, your message, and anything else you choose to include. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the issue. Email is handled through our email provider’s servers.

Please do not send us card numbers, passwords or other sensitive credentials by email. We will never ask you for them.

6. Why we process your data, and our legal basis

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to take your order, deliver your Product, provide re-downloads and handle refunds
  • Legal obligation — to keep records of sales and tax for the period required by law
  • Legitimate interests — to answer your support emails, to keep the website secure, and to prevent and investigate fraud and chargebacks, balanced against your rights and freedoms

We do not rely on consent for anything, because we do not run marketing, tracking or profiling. We do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not build behavioural profiles.

7. Marketing

We do not run a mailing list and we do not send marketing emails. The only email you will get from us is a direct reply to something you sent, or a message about an order you placed.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information for anyone else’s marketing. We have never done so and we have no plans to.

Your data is shared only with the service providers we need in order to operate:

  • Whop — payment processing, order management and digital delivery
  • Vercel — website hosting and content delivery
  • our email provider — receiving and sending support email

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the UK or the European Economic Area, the transfer is made under an appropriate safeguard such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or an adequacy decision.

10. How long we keep it

Order and transaction records are retained for as long as required by tax and accounting law, which in most jurisdictions is between 6 and 7 years from the end of the relevant financial year. Support email is kept for up to 24 months after the matter is resolved, then deleted. Server logs are retained for a short period by our host under its own retention schedule.

11. Security

The website is served only over HTTPS and sends a strict Content-Security-Policy, HTTP Strict Transport Security, and related protective headers. Because the site collects nothing, there is no customer database here to breach. Payment security is handled by Whop under the PCI DSS standard.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data ever occurred, we would notify the relevant supervisory authority and affected individuals where the law requires it.

12. Children’s privacy

Our Products are not sold to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Buyers under 18 must have the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who should be the person making the payment.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information or made a purchase without your consent, email starfnshop@gmail.com and we will delete the information and refund the purchase.

13. Your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Erase your data, where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it (note that tax law requires us to retain basic transaction records)
  • Restrict or object to our processing in certain circumstances
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk

14. Your rights under the CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, used, disclosed or sold; the right to delete personal information we hold about you; the right to correct inaccurate personal information; the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

We do not and will not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined by the CCPA and CPRA, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. Because we sell nothing, no “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” mechanism is required; if that ever changed, we would add one before making any such change.

Residents of other US states with comparable privacy laws may exercise equivalent rights by contacting us.

15. How to exercise your rights

Email starfnshop@gmail.com with the words “Privacy request” in the subject line and tell us what you would like us to do. Please write from the email address you used at checkout, or include your order reference, so that we can verify the request — we will not disclose data to someone we cannot identify as the data subject or their authorised agent.

We respond within 30 days. Exercising your rights is free; we will only charge a reasonable fee, or decline, if a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, and we will tell you why if that happens.

If your request concerns payment data held by Whop, we will tell you so and point you to Whop, since that data sits under Whop’s control.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The “last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version. If we ever introduce analytics, cookies, or any other form of tracking, we will update this policy and the Cookie Notice, and add a consent mechanism, before switching it on.

Questions about this policy

Email starfnshop@gmail.com and we will respond within 2 business days. If your question relates to a purchase, please include the order reference from your Whop receipt.
